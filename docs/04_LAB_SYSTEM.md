# Lab System

Every topic provides:

1. Guided Lab — step-by-step execution.
2. Practical Lab — partial guidance and checkpoints.
3. Challenge Lab — minimal help and independent reasoning.
4. Production Lab — realistic deployment with cost and safety controls.
5. Troubleshooting Lab — deliberately broken environment requiring diagnosis and repair.

Every lab defines objectives, prerequisites, duration, cost boundary, environment, hints, validation, cleanup, safety warnings, evidence, and rubric. Labs must not expose secrets or encourage unsafe activity. Production labs require explicit scope and teardown guidance.
