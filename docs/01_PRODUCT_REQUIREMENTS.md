# Product Requirements

## Information architecture

The primary product areas are:

- **Home** — value proposition and public discovery.
- **Learn** — paths, modules, and rooms.
- **Practice** — labs, investigations, hunts, battles, and projects.
- **Career** — portfolio, ATS, interviews, certifications, internships, and jobs.
- **Account** — dashboard, profile, notes, progress, and settings.
- **Admin** — content, operations, analytics, and audit controls.

## Catalog behavior

Public visitors can browse paths, modules, resources, labs, roles, and certifications. Launching a room requires Guest Mode or sign-in. Catalog items must show prerequisites, duration, difficulty, skills, provider, and portfolio value.

## Product quality

Every user flow needs loading, empty, error, success, recovery, keyboard, mobile, and reduced-motion states. Avoid duplicate routes and fragmented journeys. Preserve local progress during cloud outages, but never treat local storage as authorization.

## Priority

P0: secure authentication, RLS, admin authorization, AI key removal.  
P1: public catalogs, unified Learn and Practice, connected progression.  
P2: mentor, notes, analytics, certification mapping.  
P3: deployment automation, observability, and scale.
